Research Platform Demo
A two-step experience — see the platform as a patient enters data, then see exactly what the dermatologist and researcher receives.

What You Will Experience

1
Patient Entry — Complete a brief clinical check-in the way a patient would between appointments. Takes approximately 60 seconds.
2
Researcher Output — See the structured, validated, research-grade data output — BTH-QoL score, PHQ-2 result, de-identified data fields, and population-level statistics.
3
What This Makes Possible — The research infrastructure this field has been missing — now built.

About This Demo

No information you enter is collected, stored, or transmitted. This is a secure interactive preview of the Beyond the Hairline research platform. Population data shown is for demonstration purposes.
